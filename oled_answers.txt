--------------------------------
1. SIMPLE HARMONIC MOTION (SHM) 
--------------------------------

Q1: Conditions for SHM
--------------------------------
1. Restoring force F is directly
   proportional to displacement:
   F = -k * x
2. Force is always directed
   towards the mean position.

Q2: Simple Pendulum Derivation
--------------------------------
Restoring Torque:
tau = -m * g * L * sin(theta)

For small angle theta:
sin(theta) ~= theta
tau = -m * g * L * theta

Moment of inertia:
I * alpha = tau
alpha = -(m * g * L / I) * theta

For point mass bob: I = m * L^2
alpha = -(g / L) * theta

Compare alpha = -w^2 * theta:
w = sqrt(g / L)

Time Period T = 2 * pi / w
T = 2 * pi * sqrt(L / g)

Note: T is independent of mass
'm' of the bob.

Q3: Mass Attached to Spring
--------------------------------
Restoring Force F = -k * y
m * a = -k * y
a = -(k / m) * y

Comparing with a = -w^2 * y:
w = sqrt(k / m)

Time Period T = 2 * pi / w
T = 2 * pi * sqrt(m / k)

Q4: Total Energy in SHM
--------------------------------
P.E. = (1/2) * k * x^2
     = (1/2) * m * w^2 * x^2

K.E. = (1/2) * m * v^2
     = (1/2)*m*w^2*(A^2-x^2)

Total Energy E = P.E. + K.E.
E = (1/2) * m * w^2 * A^2
(Energy remains CONSTANT)

Position where K.E. = P.E.:
(1/2)*m*w^2*(A^2-x^2) = 
(1/2)*m*w^2*x^2
=> A^2 - x^2 = x^2
=> 2 * x^2 = A^2
=> x = +- A / sqrt(2)

Q5: SHM Equation Numerical
--------------------------------
Given: y = 0.5*sin(100t+3*pi/4)
m = 0.1 kg, A = 0.5 m

(i) Initial Phase:
    phi = 3*pi/4 rad = 135 deg

(ii) Total Energy:
    w = 100 rad/s
    E = (1/2) * m * w^2 * A^2
    E = 0.5*0.1*100^2*0.5^2
    E = 125 Joules

Q6: Force & Velocity Numerical
--------------------------------
m = 0.2 kg, A = 0.02 m
F_max = 0.6 N

F_max = m * w^2 * A
0.6 = 0.2 * w^2 * 0.02
w^2 = 150 => w = 12.25 rad/s

v_max = w * A = 12.25 * 0.02
v_max = 0.245 m/s

Q7: Time to Travel r/2
--------------------------------
T = 5 s, Amplitude = r
y = r * sin(w * t)
r / 2 = r * sin(2 * pi * t / 5)
1 / 2 = sin(2 * pi * t / 5)
2 * pi * t / 5 = pi / 6
t = 5 / 12 = 0.417 seconds

--------------------------------
2. WAVES & WAVE MOTION         
--------------------------------

Q8: Progressive Wave Derivation
--------------------------------
Displacement at origin (x = 0):
y(0, t) = A * sin(w * t)

Wave travels distance x with
speed v in time t' = x / v.

Displacement at position x:
y(x, t) = A * sin(w * (t - x/v))
y(x, t) = A * sin(w*t - (w/v)*x)

Since k = w / v = 2*pi / lambda:
y(x, t) = A * sin(w*t - k*x)

If wave travels in opposite
(-x) direction:
y(x, t) = A * sin(w*t + k*x)

Q9: Phase & Path Difference
--------------------------------
Phase change over 1 wavelength
(lambda) is 2*pi radians.

For path difference dx:
d_phi = (2*pi / lambda) * dx

Q10: Wave Momentum
--------------------------------
Q: Does a wave transmit linear
   momentum?
A: YES. Waves carry both energy
   and linear momentum:
   p = E / v

Q11: Pressure Wave
--------------------------------
Q: Why is longitudinal wave
   called a pressure wave?
A: It propagates as a series of
   compressions & rarefactions,
   causing periodic variation
   in local pressure & density.

Q12: Wave Phase Numerical
--------------------------------
f = 500 Hz, v = 350 m/s
lambda = v / f = 350/500 = 0.7 m

(i) d_phi = 60 deg = pi/3 rad
    dx = (lambda / 2*pi) * d_phi
    dx = (0.7 / 2*pi) * (pi/3)
    dx = 0.7 / 6 = 0.117 m

(ii) dt = 10^-3 s
    d_phi = w * dt = 2*pi*f * dt
    d_phi = 2*pi * 500 * 10^-3
    d_phi = pi rad = 180 deg

--------------------------------
3. MAGNETISM (CIRCULAR COIL)   
--------------------------------

Q13: Biot-Savart Law
--------------------------------
dB = (mu_0 / 4*pi) *
     (I * dl * sin(theta) / r^2)

Vector form:
dB = (mu_0 / 4*pi) *
     (I * dl x r_hat / r^2)

Current element I*dl:
It is the vector source of
magnetic field, analogous to
charge dq in electrostatics.

Q14: Field at Center of Coil
--------------------------------
dB = (mu_0/4*pi) * (I*dl / R^2)
B = integral(dB)
B = (mu_0 * I / (4*pi * R^2)) *
    integral(dl)

Since integral(dl) = 2*pi*R:
B = (mu_0 * I * 2*pi*R) /
    (4*pi * R^2)
B = mu_0 * I / (2 * R)

For N turns:
B = mu_0 * N * I / (2 * R)

Q15: Field on Axis of Coil
--------------------------------
B_axis = (mu_0 * N * I * R^2) /
         [2 * (R^2 + x^2)^(3/2)]

Q16: Axis Field Numerical
--------------------------------
R = 0.06 m, x = 0.06 m
I = 2.5 A, B = 6.39 * 10^-4 T

B = mu_0 * N * I * R^2 /
    [2 * (R^2 + x^2)^(3/2)]

(R^2+x^2)^(3/2) = 2^(3/2)*R^3
               = 2*sqrt(2)*R^3

B = mu_0 * N * I /
    (4 * sqrt(2) * R)

N = (B * 4 * sqrt(2) * R) /
    (mu_0 * I)
N = (6.39e-4*4*1.414*0.06) /
    (4*pi*10^-7 * 2.5)
N = 69.3 ~= 70 turns

Q17: Bohr Atom Field Numerical
--------------------------------
r = 5.1 * 10^-11 m
f = 6.8 * 10^15 rev/s

Current I = e * f
I = 1.6e-19 * 6.8e15
I = 1.088 * 10^-3 A

B = mu_0 * I / (2 * r)
B = (4*pi*10^-7 * 1.088e-3) /
    (2 * 5.1e-11)
B = 13.4 Tesla

--------------------------------
4. MODERN PHYSICS (ELECTRONS)  
--------------------------------

Q18: J.J. Thomson e/m Experiment
--------------------------------
1. Velocity Selector:
   Fe = Fm => e*E = e*v*B
   v = E / B

2. Electric Deflection:
   y = (1/2) * a * t^2
   a = e*E / m,  t = L / v

   y = (e*E*L^2) / (2*m*v^2)

   Substitute v = E / B:
   y = (e * B^2 * L^2) /
       (2 * m * E)

3. Specific Charge formula:
   e/m = (2 * y * E) /
         (B^2 * L^2)

   Or using Accelerating V:
   e/m = E^2 / (2 * V * B^2)

Q19: Parabolic Path in E-Field
--------------------------------
Horizontal: x = v * t => t = x/v
Vertical: a_y = e * E / m
y = (1/2) * a_y * t^2
y = (e*E / (2*m*v^2)) * x^2

Since e, E, m, v are constant:
y = C * x^2  (Parabola)

Q20: Radius in B-Field Numerical
--------------------------------
V = 2000 V, B = 0.02 T
m = 9.1 * 10^-31 kg
e = 1.6 * 10^-19 C

v = sqrt(2 * e * V / m)
v = sqrt(2*1.6e-19*2000/9.1e-31)
v = 2.65 * 10^7 m/s

r = m * v / (e * B)
r = (9.1e-31 * 2.65e7) /
    (1.6e-19 * 0.02)
r = 0.00753 m = 7.53 mm

Q21: Specific Charge Numerical
--------------------------------
v = 3 * 10^7 m/s, y = 0.002 m
L = 0.10 m, E = 1800 V/m

y = (e*E*L^2) / (2*m*v^2)
e/m = (2 * y * v^2) / (E * L^2)
e/m = (2 * 0.002 * (3e7)^2) /
      (1800 * 0.1^2)
e/m = 2.0 * 10^11 C/kg

Q22: Force Exerted by Beam
--------------------------------
V = 5000 V, I = 50 uA

v = sqrt(2 * e * V / m)
v = 4.19 * 10^7 m/s

Electrons per sec N_s = I / e
N_s = 50e-6 / 1.6e-19
N_s = 3.125 * 10^14 /s

Force F = N_s * m * v
F = 3.125e14 * 9.1e-31 * 4.19e7
F = 1.19 * 10^-8 Newton
